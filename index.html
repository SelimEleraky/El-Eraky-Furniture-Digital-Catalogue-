OUR STORY / SINCE 1950

Rooted in Damietta.
Made for your home.

Established in Damietta, Egypt, in 1950, El Eraky Furniture brings a long furniture-making heritage to the rooms we live in today.

Skilled artistry, chosen materials and thoughtful details come together in pieces made to be lived with. Classic influences sit alongside quieter modern lines, offering a choice of character for every room.
